# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to maintain a community that is welcoming, safe, respectful,
and equitable for all participants.

Lynx is a community-driven, open, and transparent project. We are
committed to fostering an environment that respects the dignity, rights,
and contributions of all individuals, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or personal characteristics.

Participation in the project is open to anyone acting in good faith and
in accordance with this Code of Conduct.


---

## Encouraged Behaviors

While acknowledging differences in communication styles, cultures, and
technical backgrounds, participants are expected to:

- Respect the purpose and scope of the project
- Communicate clearly, honestly, and constructively
- Treat others with professionalism and courtesy
- Respect differing viewpoints, design opinions, and experiences
- Take responsibility for their actions and contributions
- Give and accept constructive feedback in good faith
- Help maintain a productive, inclusive, and technically focused
  environment
- Act in ways that support the long-term health of the community


---

## Restricted Behaviors

The following behaviors are not permitted. Engaging in, promoting, or
threatening these behaviors constitutes a violation of this Code of
Conduct:

- **Harassment**, including persistent unwanted communication or
  disregard for clearly stated boundaries
- **Personal or character attacks**, including insulting, demeaning, or
  hostile comments
- **Discrimination or stereotyping** based on personal or immutable
  characteristics
- **Sexualized language or behavior** inappropriate to the context of the
  community
- **Disclosure of private or confidential information** without explicit
  consent
- **Threats, encouragement, or glorification of violence or harm**
- Any other conduct that reasonably threatens the safety, well-being, or
  collaborative nature of the community


---

## Other Unacceptable Conduct

The following behaviors are also considered violations of community
standards:

- **Impersonation or misleading identity**, including evading moderation
  actions
- **Failure to properly credit sources** or misrepresenting authorship
- **Promotional or commercial activity** that is unrelated to the project
  or conflicts with its non-commercial nature
- **Irresponsible or inflammatory communication** that normalizes or
  enables restricted behaviors


---

## Reporting Issues

Disagreements and technical debates may occur even when participants act
in good faith. Not every conflict constitutes a Code of Conduct violation.

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this Code of
Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers using appropriate
GitHub tools, such as:

- Private issues
- GitHub Discussions
- GitHub Security Advisories (when applicable)

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled with discretion.
Maintainers will prioritize safety and confidentiality to the extent
reasonably possible.


---

## Addressing and Repairing Harm

If a violation of this Code of Conduct is confirmed, maintainers may take
actions proportionate to the severity and impact of the incident in order
to protect the community and restore a healthy environment.

Actions may include, but are not limited to:

- Informal or formal warnings
- Requests for corrective action
- Temporary restrictions on participation
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent removal from the community in severe cases

These measures are community governance guidelines and do not constitute
legal obligations.


---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including but
not limited to:

- Repositories
- Issues and Pull Requests
- Discussions
- Code reviews
- Documentation
- Any official communication channels related to Lynx

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project
or community in public or collaborative spaces.
